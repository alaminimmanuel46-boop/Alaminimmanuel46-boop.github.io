Where to buy

Manufacturer storesSamsung, Google, Motorola and Nothing run frequent sales and trade-in deals.
Amazon, Best Buy, WalmartBest for unlocked models. Prices drop often, especially around Prime Day and Black Friday.
Swappa, Back MarketRefurbished or used phones. A one-year-old midrange phone often beats a new cheap one.
Prepaid carriersMint, Visible, Cricket and Metro sell cheap phones and sometimes bundle them with plans.

How to avoid a bad cheap phone

  • Check software updates. 4 or more years of security updates is the real value marker.
  • Look for an AMOLED or 90/120 Hz screen. It makes a cheap phone feel far nicer.
  • Get at least 128 GB storage and 6 GB RAM. Less will feel slow within a year.
  • Buy unlocked so you can pick any carrier and any deal.
  • Wait for sales. Many of these drop $50 to $100 within months of release.

Prices are typical US street prices and change often, so always check the current price before buying. Picks are based on general reputation for value, not a fresh price scan.
